# Privacy Policy for Taskova

**Effective Date:** September 4, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use **Taskova**. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application. By using Taskova, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Taskova is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, and **we do not collect, transmit, track, or store any of your personal data on developer-owned servers.**

All tasks and information you input into Taskova are stored locally on your device.

## 2. Operating System Backups

Taskova supports standard Android operating system backup features. If enabled on your device settings, Android may automatically back up your local app data (such as your task list) to your personal Google account.

- This backup process is handled entirely by your device's operating system, not by our servers.
- We (the developers) do not have access to, view, or store these backups.
- You can control, disable, or delete these backups at any time directly through your Android device's system settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Because Taskova operates locally and relies only on standard device storage and system backups, we do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third parties, analytics providers, or advertising networks.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your information at all times:

- You can delete individual tasks directly within the app.
- You can clear local app data from your device's system settings.
- Uninstalling the Taskova application will remove local app data from your device.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because our app does not transmit data to developer servers, no child's personal data is ever collected or stored by us.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
