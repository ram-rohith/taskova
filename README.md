# ⏳ Taskova: Your Time-Aware Task Manager

**Taskova** is a streamlined, time-sensitive productivity application designed to keep your daily schedule organized, prioritized, and strictly on track. Whether you are managing a busy workday, organizing personal chores, or keeping track of study assignments, Taskova offers a distraction-free environment to manage your time effectively.

We built Taskova with a focus on intuitive visual cues, automatic sorting, and mistake-free task management, ensuring that you spend less time organizing and more time actually getting things done.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **📅 Add Tasks with Precision**
  Quickly and easily create new tasks while assigning specific dates and times. Taskova ensures that every deadline, meeting, or personal goal is tracked down to the exact minute.

- **🔄 Automatic Descending Sort**
  Never lose track of your timeline. Taskova automatically organizes your to-do list by date and time in descending order. This intelligent structuring keeps your schedule logically arranged so you always know exactly what is coming up.

- **🎨 Dynamic Color-Coded Deadlines**
  Know what needs your attention at a single glance. Taskova uses a smart color-coding system that reacts to the current time:
  - 🟢 **Upcoming tasks** remain a reassuring green.
  - 🔴 **Overdue tasks** (passed their scheduled time) instantly turn red, alerting you that they require immediate attention.

- **🛑 Smart Duplicate Prevention**
  Keep your to-do list clean, accurate, and clutter-free. If you accidentally try to add a task that is already on your list, Taskova instantly catches it and alerts you with a clear error message via a seamless Snackbar notification at the bottom of your screen.

- **↩️ Safe Deletion & Instant Undo**
  Accidentally deleted an important task? Taskova provides immediate peace of mind. Whenever you remove an item, the app displays a removal status in a Snackbar notification along with a convenient **Undo** button. One tap, and your deleted task is instantly restored to your list.
